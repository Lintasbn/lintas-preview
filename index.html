Privacy Policy & PDPO

1. Independence, User Responsibility & Third-Party Providers: Lintas is an independent personal travel organizer not affiliated with any government, border authority, or third-party data provider. All uploaded QR passes and files remain under the user's sole control and responsibility, not Lintas. Live traffic maps, flight statuses, currency rates, weather forecasts, Halal eatery maps, and translations are supplied by external providers (Google Maps, Waze, Flightradar24, FlightAware, ExchangeRate-API, Open-Meteo, Aladhan, and MyMemory); Lintas makes no guarantees regarding accuracy and assumes no liability for inaccurate third-party information.

2. Storage, Session Grace & Retention (PDPO Compliance): Guest Mode stores passes strictly on your local device. Google Sign-In encrypts and syncs compressed QR passes to your private Firebase account with offline availability. Active sessions remain unlocked for 15 minutes across app closes unless Face ID / Fingerprint protection is enabled (which verifies biometrics on every cold open and after timeout). Tapping Sign Out immediately clears your session and removes biometric access. Trips and passes auto-delete 30 days after your trip end date.

3. Location Privacy: Lintas never stores or tracks your location history. Login-screen weather and Solat prayer times look up approximate city location via Open-Meteo and Aladhan APIs without saving coordinates.

4. Prohibited Documents & Automated Filter: Users must not upload passports, national identity cards (IC), or bank cards. Lintas runs a multi-layer on-device check before upload and blocks Bruneian or foreign passports and IDs.

5. Immediate Deletion: You can permanently delete all stored trips, passes, and your account at any time via Account & Privacy → Delete Account & Wipe All Data.

Register Biometric Access?

Optional: Verify with Face ID or Fingerprint whenever you open Lintas or after the 15-minute auto-lock. If you skip, your session stays signed in for 15 minutes across app closes.
