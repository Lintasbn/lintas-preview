Privacy Policy & PDPO

1. Purpose & Independence: Lintas is an independent personal travel organizer designed to store non-sensitive QR codes (such as EES, MDAC, and boarding passes). Lintas is not affiliated with any government or immigration authority.

2. Storage & Sync (PDPO Compliance): Guest Mode stores passes strictly on your local device. Google Sign-In encrypts and syncs compressed QR passes to your private Firebase account with offline caching, and automatically deletes trips and passes after 30 days.

3. Location & Ambient Weather/Solat: Lintas never stores or uploads your location to any server. If you optionally tap Check local weather & Solat times on the login screen, coordinates are used once in browser memory to fetch Open-Meteo weather and Aladhan prayer times, then discarded.

4. Prohibited Documents & Automated Filter: Users must not upload passports, national identity cards (IC), or bank cards. Lintas runs an on-device check before upload and blocks images resembling passports or IDs.

5. Immediate Deletion: You can permanently delete all stored trips, passes, and your account at any time via Account & Privacy → Delete Account & Wipe All Data.
