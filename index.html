Privacy Policy & PDPO

1. Purpose & Independence: Lintas is an independent personal travel organizer designed to store non-sensitive QR codes (such as EES, MDAC, and boarding passes). Lintas is not affiliated with any government or immigration authority.

2. Local Device Storage (PDPO Compliance): All uploaded images and trip folders are stored locally inside your device's browser storage (IndexedDB). Lintas does not upload, view, or store your uploaded images on external servers.

3. Prohibited Documents: Users must not upload sensitive personal identification documents, including passport biodata pages, national identity cards (IC), or financial cards.

4. Authentication & Advertising: Optional Google Sign-In uses Firebase Authentication solely to verify your session. The mobile app version may display non-intrusive banner advertisements via Google AdMob, which may use device advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

5. Data Deletion: You can permanently delete all stored trips, passes, and your account at any time via Account & Privacy → Delete Account & Wipe All Local Data.
