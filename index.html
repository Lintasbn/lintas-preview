Privacy Policy & PDPO

1. Independence & User Responsibility: Lintas is an independent personal travel organizer not affiliated with any government or border authority. All uploaded files remain under your sole control and responsibility; Lintas assumes no liability for user-stored content.

2. Storage, Session Grace & Retention (PDPO Compliance): Guest Mode stores passes strictly on your local device. Google Sign-In encrypts and syncs compressed QR passes to your private Firebase account with offline caching. Active sessions remain unlocked for 15 minutes across app closes unless Face ID / Fingerprint protection is enabled (which verifies biometrics on every cold open and after timeout). Tapping Sign Out immediately clears your session and removes biometric access. Trips and passes auto-delete 30 days after your trip end date.

3. Location Privacy: Lintas never stores or tracks your location history. Login-screen weather and Solat prayer times look up approximate city location via Open-Meteo and Aladhan APIs without saving coordinates.

4. Prohibited Documents & Automated Filter: Users must not upload passports, national identity cards (IC), or bank cards. Lintas runs a multi-layer on-device check before upload and blocks Bruneian or foreign passports and IDs.

5. Immediate Deletion: You can permanently delete all stored trips, passes, and your account at any time via Account & Privacy → Delete Account & Wipe All Data.

Register Biometric Access?

Optional: Verify with Face ID or Fingerprint whenever you open Lintas or after the 15-minute auto-lock. If you skip, your session stays signed in for 15 minutes across app closes.
