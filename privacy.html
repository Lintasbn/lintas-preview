Privacy Policy & PDPO Disclosure

App Name: Lintas — Travel QR & Border Pass Wallet

1. Purpose & Independence

Lintas is an independent personal travel organizer designed to store non-sensitive QR codes and travel receipts (such as EES, MDAC, and boarding passes). Lintas is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government or immigration authority.

2. Local Device Storage (PDPO Compliance)

All uploaded QR images and trip folders are stored locally inside your device's browser/app storage (IndexedDB). Lintas does not upload, transmit, view, or store your uploaded images on external cloud servers.

3. Prohibited Sensitive Documents

Users must not upload sensitive personal identification documents, including passport biodata pages, national identity cards (IC), or financial/bank cards.

4. Authentication & Advertising

Optional Google Sign-In uses Google Firebase Authentication solely to verify your user session. The Android mobile version may display non-intrusive banner advertisements via Google AdMob, which may collect device advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Deletion & User Control

You can permanently delete all stored trips, passes, and your account at any time inside the app by opening Settings (⚙️) → Delete Account & Wipe All Local Data, or by clearing the app's storage on your device.
